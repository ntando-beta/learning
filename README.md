# 🧠 FlipGame

A **Unity-based library game** where players flip cards to reveal images of books, match pairs, and answer questions related to the library theme.  
Each level challenges memory, logic, and knowledge while keeping the gameplay fun and educational!

---

## 🎮 Play Online  
👉 [Play FlipGame](https://ntando-beta.github.io/FlipGame/)

---

## 🕹️ Gameplay Overview
Flip the cards to find matching pairs of books.  
Once all are matched, you’ll reveal a library-related question beneath — answer correctly to progress to the next level.  
Earn points for right answers, lose points for wrong ones, and track your score on the main scoreboard.

---

## ⚙️ Features
- 🧩 Multiple levels with increasing difficulty  
- 🏆 Scoreboard and reset options  
- 🚪 Quit and restart anytime  
- 💡 Educational gameplay themed around library knowledge
